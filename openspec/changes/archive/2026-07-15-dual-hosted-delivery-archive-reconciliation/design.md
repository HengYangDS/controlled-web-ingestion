# Design

## Scope

This is a bounded reconciliation carrier for the official archive operation.
It covers the claim path change from an active closure carrier to its archive
and the canonical requirement fused by OpenSpec. GitHub and GitLab publication
remain governed by the existing canonical requirements and publication
reference, not by this carrier.

## Completion

The carrier is complete when the claim is bound to the archive, strict
validation and the local proof pass, and this carrier itself is archived before
candidate integration. Archival is not hosted publication evidence.
