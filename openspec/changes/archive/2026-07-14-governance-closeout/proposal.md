# Governance closeout and durable delivery readiness

## Why

The published topology has bounded functional evidence, but its closeout has
not yet reconciled local, runtime, governance, hosted-CI, and protected-merge
facts into one auditable record. Its former ETHOS branch-role mapping also
could not mint an owned Work Lane after the topology branch was merged and
retired. Delivery readiness must be repaired without treating a personal Mac,
a historical runner, or a prior pipeline as a continuing CI capability.

## What Changes

- **governance-closeout:** Record the bounded closeout receipt, chronicle,
  migration map, admission packet, and corrected delivery train as repository
  material; the canonical governance-closeout capability is already present.
- **governance-closeout:** Preserve the existing authority boundary: no new
  target, data class, runtime topology, persisted body, MCP, Hermes/Open WebUI,
  or RAG sink follows from this closeout.

## Affected capabilities

- `governance-closeout`: subject=controlled-web-ingestion:governance-closeout;
  reuse=extend; change=add; facet:lifecycle=closeout;
  facet:surface=documentation-and-evidence; facet:authority=repository-governance

## Out of Scope

- No Firecrawl runtime execution, image change, daemon, target expansion, or
  direct RAG delivery.
- No registration, modification, reuse, or removal of a GitLab runner.
- No direct write to protected `main`.
